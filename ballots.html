How to read this page
  • Nevada mails a ballot to every active registered voter. Voters can mail it back or drop it off, vote in person during early voting (Oct 17–30), or vote in person on Election Day (Nov 3).
  • Mail ballots returned counts mail ballots counties have received back. In person counts people who checked in to vote at a polling place. When someone votes in person, their mail ballot is canceled, so the same voter is not counted twice.
  • Party is the voter's party registration, not how they voted. “Other” is everyone not registered as a Democrat or Republican (nonpartisan, Independent American, Libertarian and smaller parties), grouped the same way the Secretary of State does.
  • Party lead (map colors, “D +3.2 pts”) = Democratic share minus Republican share of all ballots received. “Other” is part of the total but not the color.
  • % of registered compares ballots received with the Secretary of State's count of active registered voters (Nov 1, 2026 report).
  • Mail ballots postmarked by Nov 3 count if they arrive by Nov 7, so the mail numbers keep growing after Election Day. This page never estimates or projects anything. All times are Pacific Time (PT).
