All results are unofficial until the county canvasses (Washoe: Nov 13, 2026) and state certification. This page only shows numbers present in the SOS feed; it never estimates or projects. The SOS feed reports counties received/complete, not precincts or expected vote. The data file is refreshed from the SOS feed about every 2 minutes and this page re-checks it about every 60 seconds. Nevada has no U.S. Senate race in 2026. All times are Pacific Time (PT).
