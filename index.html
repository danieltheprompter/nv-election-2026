How to read this page
  • Pick a race below: choose Statewide offices, U.S. House or Questions 6 & 7, then tap a race. Every statewide race, all four U.S. House districts and both ballot questions are there, each with its county map.
  • Bars and percentages show each choice's share of the votes counted so far. The leader is shown in bold.
  • The map colors each county by who is ahead there. A lighter shade means a closer margin; gray means no results yet. Tap or click a county for its numbers. To zoom any map, use its + / − buttons, pinch on a phone, or hold Ctrl (⌘ on a Mac) and scroll; drag to move around a zoomed map.
  • “Counties reporting” is how many counties have sent results to the Secretary of State. It does not mean all their ballots are counted — Nevada keeps counting mail ballots for days.
  • This page checks for new numbers every minute on its own, so you never need to refresh. All times are Pacific Time (PT).
  • Everything here is unofficial until counties finish their canvass (Washoe: Nov 13, 2026) and the state certifies results. This page never estimates or projects winners.
